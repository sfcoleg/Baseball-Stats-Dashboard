Yesterday against the Twins, Jacob Misiorowski became the first pitcher this season to reach 200 strikeouts, and the second-fastest to hit that mark in MLB history, doing it in just 129.1 innings.

The right-hander has been nearly unhittable all year. His fastball averages close to 101 mph, and he pairs that velocity with elite control: a 40% strikeout rate against just a 6% walk rate, and a 1.79 ERA to show for it. It's no surprise he's the current NL Cy Young favorite.

Misiorowski is also a testament to how good the Brewers' scouting and player development really is. Milwaukee is running a bottom-third payroll and still has the best record in baseball, same as last year. Come October, we'll find out exactly what he and this Brewers team are capable of.
